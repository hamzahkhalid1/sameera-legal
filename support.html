Common questions

How do I find recipes I can make?

Add what's in your kitchen on the Pantry tab, by typing or by tapping the microphone and saying it. Home then shows what's ready to cook, what works with a swap, and what you're one or two things away from.

How do I post a recipe?

Tap the + in the middle of the tab bar, add a photo, and describe how you made it. BOCA writes it up for you to check before it's posted.

How do I report a recipe, a comment, or a cook?

Tap the ··· on the recipe or next to the comment and choose Report. You can also block a cook from the same menu. We review reports within 24 hours.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and we'll email you a link to set a new one.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. Your account, pantry, and the recipes and photos you posted are deleted right away. You can also email us to do it for you.
