Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

SAMEERA helps you cook with what you have. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We have tried to write it in plain language.

1. Who we are

SAMEERA is operated by the SAMEERA team (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at hamzahkhalid000@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

You can use the cookbook without an account. We collect information in three ways:

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, a username, and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form — we cannot read your password.
  • Your kitchen: the ingredients in your pantry, your grocery list, your daily menu, and the recipes you rate.
  • Food preferences: diets (such as vegan or gluten-free), allergies, favorite cuisines, ingredients you want to avoid, and how long you like to cook. Allergy and diet information can relate to your health, so we treat it as sensitive and use it only to filter and sort recipes for you.
  • Settings: for example your theme and vegetarian setting.

Information created as you use the app

  • A history of the menus you confirm — which recipes and the total calories for each day — used for your dashboard and recommendations.
  • Technical information your browser sends whenever you load any website (such as your IP address and device type), which our hosting and database providers may log for security and reliability.

Voice input

If you use “Say your ingredients”, your device or browser's built-in speech recognition turns your voice into text. On iPhone this is Apple's speech service, and in some browsers it is run by the browser maker (for example Google for Chrome). We do not receive, record, or store your audio — only the words recognized as ingredients are added to your pantry. Your device's own privacy settings and the browser's terms govern how that audio is handled.

3. How we use your information

  • To run the app: save your pantry, menu, grocery list, ratings, and settings, and sync them across your devices.
  • To personalize what you see: hide recipes that contain your allergens or don’t fit your diet, show your favorite cuisines first, and suggest recipes that match your pantry.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account, such as confirming your email address or resetting a password.

Anonymous, aggregated data. We may combine information about how the app is used — for example which cuisines, recipes, or ingredients are popular, or what share of users follow a particular diet — into statistics that do not identify you, and we may share or sell those statistics to other businesses. This data is aggregated and de-identified: it never includes your name, email address, username, user ID, IP address, or anything else that could reasonably be used to identify you, and we do not share figures for groups so small that a person could be singled out. We require anyone who receives it not to try to re-identify individuals.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not currently use advertising or analytics trackers. If that changes we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

4. Where your data is stored and who handles it

We rely on a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — our database and sign-in provider. It stores your account and the information in section 2 when you have an account.
  • Our hosting provider — serves the website and app files to your device.
  • Pexels — recipe photos are loaded directly from Pexels' servers, so Pexels can see your IP address and device details when a photo loads. Photographers are credited on each recipe.
  • Google Fonts — the app's typefaces are loaded from Google, which can see your IP address when they load.

Without an account, your pantry, menu, grocery list, preferences, and settings stay only on your own device (in your browser's local storage). When you create an account, we copy that information to your account so it follows you.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of people using the app.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage (not advertising cookies) to remember your guest data, theme, and whether you have finished the first-run setup, and our authentication provider uses it to keep you signed in. The app also caches files on your device so it can load quickly and work with a weak connection. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings, though that will sign you out and remove guest data stored only on that device.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account yourself at any time in Settings → Delete account, or by emailing us. When you do, we delete your profile data, including your pantry, menu history, grocery list, ratings, and food preferences, except where we must keep something to comply with the law or resolve disputes. Backups are overwritten on a regular schedule.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • see and get a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong — most of it you can edit yourself in the app, including Settings → Food preferences;
  • delete your account and your data — you can do this yourself in Settings → Delete account;
  • object to or limit certain uses of your information, and withdraw consent where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email hamzahkhalid000@gmail.com from the address on your account (deleting your account is instant in Settings). We will respond within a reasonable time, and no later than the period your local law requires. You can also remove your food preferences at any time by clearing them in Settings, and you may complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Children

SAMEERA is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact hamzahkhalid000@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use industry-standard protections: data is encrypted while it travels between your device and our servers, passwords are hashed, and our database is configured so each person can read and change only their own profile. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us right away if you think your account was accessed without permission.

10. International users

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a significant change we will tell you in the app or by email, and we will always change the “Last updated” date above.

12. Contact us

Questions, requests, or concerns about your privacy: hamzahkhalid000@gmail.com. You can also read our Terms of Service.
